German Electricity Market Analysis — Solar PV & Day-Ahead Prices

I completed a Python and Jupyter Notebook project analyzing German-Luxembourg day-ahead electricity prices using ENTSO-E data. The project focuses on how solar PV generation affects intraday price patterns, negative price hours, and the market value captured by solar assets.

The analysis uses ENTSO-E day-ahead price data and solar generation data for the German-Luxembourg bidding zone, with a focus on the merit order effect, negative prices, and solar capture rates.

Key findings

  • Daytime solar generation became increasingly linked to lower day-ahead prices after the crisis period.
  • Negative price hours became much more common and were mostly concentrated around midday.
  • Solar capture rates weakened after the energy crisis, showing stronger signs of solar cannibalisation.
  • The 2021–2022 energy crisis distorted the usual solar-price relationship because fuel costs dominated price formation.

Tools: Python, pandas, matplotlib, Jupyter Notebook, ENTSO-E API, entsoe-py
