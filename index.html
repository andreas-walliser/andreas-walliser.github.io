About

I was born in Singapore and grew up in Thailand, where I completed my international education before moving to Germany to study mechanical engineering. I was drawn to engineering by an interest in problem-solving, energy and sustainability.

During a study break in Thailand, I focused on investing and trading in the Thai market. I later returned to Germany for an M.Sc. in Sustainable Energy Supply at RWTH Aachen because I wanted to deepen my understanding of the energy sector from a broader technical perspective. The programme combined mechanical engineering, electrical engineering and resource-related energy topics, which matched my interest in energy systems and sustainability.

I enjoy working with both technical and commercial questions. Data, coding and structured analysis are tools I like to use to understand complex problems and develop clear, logical solutions.

Project 01 / Completed

German Electricity Market Analysis — Solar PV & Day-Ahead Prices

An exploratory Python and Jupyter Notebook analysis of German-Luxembourg day-ahead electricity prices and solar generation from 2020 to 2026 year-to-date, using ENTSO-E data.

  • Daytime solar generation became increasingly linked to lower prices after the energy-crisis period.
  • Negative-price hours became more common and were concentrated mainly around midday.
  • Solar capture rates weakened after the crisis period.
